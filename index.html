About

Research at the intersection of vision, language and multimodal intelligence.

I am an Institute Post-Doctoral Fellow in the Department of Artificial Intelligence at IIT Kharagpur. I completed my PhD in Computer Science & Engineering from IIEST Shibpur in 2025, with a thesis focused on deep learning-driven frameworks for multimodal social media content analysis.

My work combines machine learning, deep learning, computer vision and NLP to build robust systems for multimodal content understanding, recognition, classification, segmentation and explainable decision-making.
